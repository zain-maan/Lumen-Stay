## Project overview

Boutique hotels pay 15 to 25% commission on every OTA booking, often because their own site looks dated and makes booking hard. Lumen Stay needed a website that matched the feeling of the resort, and a booking path short enough that guests would not bother going anywhere else.

The hero pairs a cinematic cliff-pool shot with an availability bar, so dates and guests can be entered straight away. Below it, a pinned, scroll-driven tour slides through the four villas, and a trip planner suggests a villa and experiences for a honeymoon, wellness break or family trip. Each villa has its own page with a live availability calendar and running total, and a four-step booking flow takes the guest from dates to confirmation, with an AI concierge on hand throughout.

## Key features

1. **Instant availability search**: dates and guests in one bar, right in the hero.
2. **Scroll-driven villa tour**: the villas glide past horizontally as you scroll.
3. **Trip planner**: choose the kind of trip and the recommendations change instantly.
4. **Live availability calendar**: booked nights blocked, price updates as you pick dates.
5. **Four-step booking**: dates, villa, extras and details, with transparent pricing at every step.
6. **AI concierge**: answers questions about villas, transfers, dining and policies at any hour.
